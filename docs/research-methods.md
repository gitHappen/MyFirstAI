# Research methods

**Observed:** personally seen, photographed, counted, or measured. Preserve original notes and record dates only when known.

**Researched:** supported by a specific external source. Include the author or organization, title, URL, and access date. External information must not be presented as a personal observation.

**Inferred:** a tentative interpretation with the reasoning and uncertainty stated.

**Unknown:** not yet established. Candidate animal groups do not establish that a species was observed or correctly identified.

Keep original notebook pages and photos so transcriptions can be checked. Record corrections transparently in Git history. Use group-level identification until evidence supports something more specific. Explain sampling limits, identification uncertainty, observer bias, and environmental confounders before interpreting patterns.

Observe gently: avoid damaging habitats, disturbing nests, or handling dangerous wildlife. Prefer non-invasive observation. Publish only broad habitat descriptions; remove photo location metadata and do not expose home coordinates.

The website is a documentation scaffold. No completed studies, counts, original photos, or biographical discoveries are claimed by its initial contents. Ecosystem and evolutionary features remain future work pending documented evidence and sources. Application submissions must follow current institutional rules and accurately disclose assistance.
